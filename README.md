Key Business Observations
Mumbai dominance: 1,060 transactions (51% of volume). Branch C (likely Mumbai flagship) alone represents ~50% of activity.
Category leader: "Food and beverages" is the clear winner (706 invoices, far ahead of Electronic accessories at 378).
Payment behavior: Extremely cash-heavy (1,284 transactions / ~62%). Digital (Ewallet + Credit card) together only ~38%. Opportunity for cashless incentives.
Customer mix: Normal customers slightly outnumber Members (1,107 vs 958). No strong loyalty skew visible.
Gender skew: Data shows 1,491 Male vs 574 Female transactions. Investigate if this is assortment-driven, location-driven, or data collection bias.
Margin reality: ~4.8% is thin but typical for high-volume retail. Focus must stay on volume, basket size, and reducing COGS leakage.
Satisfaction: 6.41 average rating leaves material headroom. Lowest-rated categories/times should be prioritized for operational review.
